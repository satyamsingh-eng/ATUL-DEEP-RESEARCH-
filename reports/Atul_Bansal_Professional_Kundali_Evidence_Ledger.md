## Reader controls

- **FACT** — direct source support, constrained to source wording and date.
- **INFERENCE** — explicitly bounded synthesis across facts.
- **UNRESOLVED / vendor signal** — a direct-conversation question or third-party record that is not promoted.
- **SOURCE DRIFT / WITHHELD** — historical wording retained from a report-safe page capture that a current public page no longer reproduces. It cannot support a current role.
- **Confidence** qualifies the *specific proposition*, not the target’s overall profile.
