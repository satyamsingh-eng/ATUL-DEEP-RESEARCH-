## How to read this ledger

- **FACT** = direct source support, limited to the wording and date shown.
- **INFERENCE** = constrained synthesis; it does not create a relationship, authority, motive, or access claim.
- **WITHHELD** = material question with no sufficient proof. It stays unresolved.
- **SOURCE DRIFT / WITHHELD** = an earlier captured page wording that a current public page no longer reproduces. It is historical context only, never a current-role claim.
- **Vendor signal** = third-party database observation. It cannot promote a current role, historic title, authority, contact permission, relationship, or investment claim.

## Relationship interpretation rule

- appearing on the same chapter/board/platform page;
- historic board/company/transaction co-listing;
- a company investor list;
- institutional or regional overlap;
- a data-vendor record;
- page presence or absence;
- acquisition/customer/partner context; or
- a person’s independently verified domain expertise.
