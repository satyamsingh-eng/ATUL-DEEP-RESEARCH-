## Turn operating context into approved, traceable business work

SARVAX helps wealth and advisory teams move from client context to structured action—without asking people to trust a black box.

| Step | Intent |
|---|---|
| **01 · Capture context** | Meetings, documents, and operational messages. |
| **02 · Structure work** | Clear action packets, owners, and next steps. |
| **03 · Apply approval** | Named review for risk-sensitive decisions. |
| **04 · Complete safely** | Controlled downstream work with traceability. |

## The problem

Critical work in wealth and advisory operations is fragmented across conversations, client documents, messages, trackers, and core systems. Point note-takers stop at a summary. Generic chat does not complete governed work.

## Execution, not another interface

SARVAX is designed to turn operating context into a structured, reviewable action path. Approval is part of the product. Where a person must decide, the workflow makes that responsibility explicit.

## Built for repeatable operations

The aim is a trusted workflow pattern—not a one-off automation demonstration.
